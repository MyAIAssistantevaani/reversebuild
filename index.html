THE REVERSEBUILD METHOD

Don't just ask whether an idea is good. Understand how it could work.

ReverseBuild is designed around a simple principle: study businesses solving a similar problem, understand the mechanics behind their model, and then work backward from your own target.

01

Find comparable businesses

Identify businesses with meaningful similarities in customer, problem, product, market, or business model.

02

Study their strategy

Examine customer positioning, product structure, pricing, acquisition channels, traffic sources, conversion paths, and growth mechanisms.

03

Work backward from your goal

Turn a revenue target into customer, conversion, lead, traffic, and acquisition requirements using explicit assumptions and calculations.

04

Decide what to change

Separate strategies worth keeping from areas that deserve improvement or alternative experiments.

THE DECISION LAYER

Not everything needs to be copied.

A successful business can provide useful evidence without providing a perfect blueprint. ReverseBuild separates observation from recommendation.

Each strategic component can be classified as KEEP, IMPROVE, or CHANGE, with the reasoning and assumptions made visible.

KEEP

The comparable strategy appears relevant to the user's circumstances and is worth considering without major changes.

IMPROVE

The underlying mechanism may be useful, but there is an opportunity to test a stronger execution.

CHANGE

The comparable approach may not fit the user's constraints, creating a reason to test another route.
