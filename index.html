THE REVERSEBUILD METHOD

Don't just ask whether an idea is good. Understand how it could work.

ReverseBuild uses a structured approach: define the outcome, understand the customer, map the business mechanics, work backward through the numbers, and identify the assumptions worth testing.

01

Define the target

Start with a measurable outcome instead of beginning with random marketing activities.

02

Understand the customer

Identify the customer, problem, current alternative, buying trigger, and desired outcome.

03

Map the business mechanics

Examine value proposition, product, business model, pricing, acquisition, traffic, funnel, and growth.

04

Work backward

Translate the revenue target into customer, conversion, traffic, and testing requirements.

NINE STRATEGIC DIMENSIONS

01

Target Customer

Who buys, who uses, and which customer segment the initial strategy should focus on.

02

Value Proposition

The problem being solved and the outcome the customer expects.

03

Product

Core functionality, customer experience, differentiation, and potential gaps.

04

Business Model

How the business captures value and possible monetization approaches.

05

Pricing

Pricing assumptions, customer value, positioning, and revenue scenarios.

06

07

Traffic & Demand

The journey from visitor to lead, customer, purchase, and retention.

09

Growth Strategy

THE DECISION LAYER

Not everything needs to be copied.

A successful business can provide useful evidence without providing a perfect blueprint. ReverseBuild separates observations, assumptions, and recommendations.
